Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026
The short version: this site doesn't track you, doesn't use cookies and doesn't have accounts. The only personal info I get is what you choose to type into the contact box, and I only use it to reply to you.

Who runs this site

This site is run by Wheat, the creator behind @wheatsellz0 on TikTok. You can reach me through the contact box or by DM on TikTok.

What I collect

Only what you send through the contact box:

  • your TikTok username
  • the topic you pick (promo, collab, question or something else)
  • your email address, only if you choose to add one
  • your message

I use this to read your message and reply to you, for example about a promo or a question on a guide. That's it. I don't sell it, share it for marketing, or add you to any mailing list.

How your message gets to me

The contact box uses a form service called Web3Forms to deliver your message to my email inbox. Web3Forms handles the message to send it, under its own privacy policy.

What I don't do

  • No cookies and no tracking or advertising scripts.
  • No analytics right now. If I ever add them, I'll use a privacy-friendly, cookie-free tool and update this page first.
  • Fonts are hosted on this site, so loading a page doesn't send your details to Google.

Stored on your own device

If you take a mini test, your best score is saved in your browser's local storage so you can see it next time. It never leaves your device, and you can clear it any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Hosting and links

Like any website, the company that hosts this site may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to keep it running and secure. The guides link to other sites like YouTube and GitHub, and those sites have their own privacy policies.

How long I keep messages

Only as long as I need them to reply or to sort out a promo. I delete old messages at least once a year.

Your rights, and deleting your data

You can ask what I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Just send a message through the contact box (or DM me on TikTok) saying "delete my data", and I'll do it within 30 days. If you're in the UK or EU you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, that's the ICO).

Under 13?

Please ask a parent or guardian before sending a message, and don't include personal details like your address or phone number.

Changes

If anything here changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
